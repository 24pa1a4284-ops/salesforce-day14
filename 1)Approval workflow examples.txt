 1)Approval workflow examples
 A)
    1. Course Creation Approval
    Faculty creates course request.
    HOD reviews the request.
    Principal gives final approval.
    After approval, course becomes active.
    If rejected, request returns for correction.

 2 example
 
 2. Faculty Leave Request
Faculty submits leave request.
HOD checks leave details.
Principal approves the request.
Leave status becomes approved.
If rejected, faculty receives notification.
